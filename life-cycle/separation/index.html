Separation: what to review in your estate plan

A separation does not by itself cancel your documents. Your spouse may still be your agent, your beneficiary, and your executor. At the same time, changes made while a case is pending may be restricted, so this is a moment to coordinate carefully.

Schedule a ConsultationAdd to your review list

Worth reviewing

  • Who is currently named in your will, powers of attorney, and beneficiary forms
  • What your family law attorney or any court order allows you to change right now
  • Securing copies of documents and account information
  • A short-term plan for your children if something happens to you

Documents to look at first

For this life event, these are the documents most often affected. Your own list depends on your family and what you have already signed.

  • Health care power of attorney and living will
  • Financial power of attorney
  • Beneficiary designations (retirement, insurance, bank accounts)
  • Your will
  • How your home and accounts are titled

When to act

Before making changes.
