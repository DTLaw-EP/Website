A New Child: what to review in your estate plan

A child cannot manage an inheritance, and if both parents were gone, a court would decide who raises your child unless you have said otherwise. This is the single most common reason parents come in.

Schedule a ConsultationAdd to your review list

Worth reviewing

  • A guardian, and a backup, named for your child
  • Whether a trust should hold money for your child until an age you choose
  • Beneficiaries on life insurance and retirement accounts
  • Enough life insurance to replace income and cover your child’s needs
  • Who controls any education savings account

Documents to look at first

For this life event, these are the documents most often affected. Your own list depends on your family and what you have already signed.

  • Guardian nominations for children
  • Your will
  • Revocable living trust
  • Beneficiary designations (retirement, insurance, bank accounts)
  • Life and disability insurance

When to act

Right away.
