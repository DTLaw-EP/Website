About the attorney

Yung Truong, attorney

Yung Truong is an Illinois attorney and the founder of DT Law in Naperville. Admitted in Illinois in 2021, she practiced civil litigation and Illinois probate and estate planning, and worked as a Registered Behavior Technician with children. She now focuses on estate planning for families, including those with special needs.

What shapes the practice

Most estate planning treats a disability as an exception to plan around. Here, it is the plan. Working with children on the autism spectrum showed Yung that her calling sits at the intersection of caregiving and advocating for children with special needs. That background shapes every document this firm drafts: language a family can actually act on, trustees who understand what “supplemental needs” means day to day, and a guardianship timeline that starts before it becomes an emergency.
