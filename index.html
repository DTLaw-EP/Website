"An estate plan that adapts and grows with your family and its needs."

An agile estate plan is the answer to those nagging questions that keep you up at night: How would my loved ones be cared for when I'm no longer around?

At DT Law, we do not simply plan for the distant future but the changing landscape of your family's needs. As your children grow and your family dynamic evolves, we build and adapt with you.

Practice areas

Four ways we help, in the order families usually need them

01

Basic estate planning

A will, financial and health care powers of attorney, and a living will — drafted so they work with your family's special needs plan instead of against it.

Where every family starts

02

Special needs trusts

A trust that holds an inheritance, settlement, or life insurance payout for your loved ones without counting against SSI or Medicaid resource limits.

Before any inheritance or settlement is finalized

03

Education advocacy

Authorization and records access so we can sit in on IEP and 504 meetings with you, plus create a clear roadmap of your rights and the district's deadlines.

Any time school issues arise, especially transition years

04

Guardianship

Your legal authority as a parent ends the day your child turns 18. We file for guardianship — or the least restrictive alternative — well ahead of that birthday.

Beginning 6–12 months before age 18

About DT Law

A practice built from both sides of the table

After six years of practice in civil litigation, attorney Yung Truong took time away from the law to work as a Registered Behavior Technician. While working with children on the autism spectrum, she discovered that her true calling lies at the intersection between caregiving and advocating for children with special needs.

"Most estate planning treats a disability as an exception to plan around. Here, it's the plan."

That background shapes every document this firm drafts: language a family can actually act on, trustees who understand what "supplemental needs" means day to day, and a guardianship timeline that starts before it becomes an emergency.
